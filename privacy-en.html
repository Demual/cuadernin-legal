Privacy Policy

Cuadernín app (com.jrsoft.notascolores) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In one sentence: Cuadernín keeps your notes and checklists inside your phone. There are no accounts and no servers of its own, and what you write never leaves your phone unless you share it, save a backup or export it. Apart from that, the only data that leaves it is the technical and advertising data Google collects to serve the ads, as explained below.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JR Soft, trading name of Javier Román Sáez, developer of the Cuadernín app. You can get in touch at demual@gmail.com.

What this app does NOT do

  • There is no sign-up, account, username or password for the app. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number, a name or your location.
  • It does not upload your notes. Your notes, checklists, photos, labels and reminders are stored on your phone. This app has no servers of its own: JR Soft never receives anything you write.
  • The app includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics and no Crashlytics). Google's advertising SDK does send its own technical data, explained below.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. To show and measure ads, Google's ads SDK (AdMob) collects device identifiers, as explained in this policy. Some US privacy laws may treat that as “selling” or “sharing” personal data (see “US residents”).
  • It does not ask for permission to use the camera, your photos and files, your contacts or your location. Photos for your notes are picked with the system photo picker or taken with the phone's camera app, and files are opened and saved through Android's own screens, only the ones you choose.
  • Your notes are not included in Android's backup to Google's cloud.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and vibration, to alert you to your reminders. On Android 13 or later, the notification permission is requested when you set your first reminder.
  • Alarms and reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), so that reminders arrive at the exact time. On Android 14 or later, only if you grant it yourself from Settings → Exact-time reminders; without it, reminders still arrive, with a few minutes' leeway.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), to schedule your reminders again after the phone restarts or the app is updated.
  • Fingerprint or face recognition (USE_BIOMETRIC), only if you lock the app or a note. The check is done by Android, with the phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password: the app never receives them, it only learns whether it was unlocked or not.
  • Internet, network state and advertising identifier (AD_ID and the permissions of Android's advertising services), which the advertising needs (see below). Your notes do not leave through them.

Data that stays only on your phone

This information is kept in the app's private storage (a database and a photo folder). It disappears if you uninstall the app, if you clear its data from Android settings or if you use Settings → Delete all my data.

  • Your notes and checklists: the title, the text and its formatting, the checklist items, the colour, the emoji, the labels, the dates and whether they are pinned, archived or locked.
  • The photos you add to your notes. A new copy of each one is saved, without its metadata (for example, the place where it was taken).
  • Your reminders, which are scheduled on the phone itself, and your templates.
  • The trash: what you delete is kept for 30 days and then removed.
  • Your settings: language, theme, text size, sort order, lock, the automatic backup folder, what each widget shows and the other options.
  • A photo or file you share with Cuadernín from another app, or that you choose to import or to restore a backup, is copied to the app's temporary storage so it can be read, and that copy is deleted when it is done.

Reminders, widgets, the lock and search all work inside the phone, offline.

Backups and switching phones. Android's cloud backup (the one in your Google account) is turned off for this app's data: none of it is uploaded. Android does keep a list of the apps you had installed in your account so it can reinstall them, but Google manages that list and it contains no data from this app.

If, while setting up a new phone, you copy your data directly from the old one, with or without a cable, the app's data (your notes, their photos and your settings) may be moved to the new phone. You ask for this transfer and Android carries it out, using its own tool or your phone maker's, under their own terms. According to Android's documentation, it goes from one phone to the other without going through servers. It never goes through ours: JR Soft receives nothing.

Whether it happens depends on your Android version and phone maker (it doesn't happen on Android 8 or earlier), so we can't guarantee it. If you set up the new phone from your Google account backup without the old phone, this app's data won't come back, because it isn't in that backup.

Anything that only makes sense on the old phone is not transferred, such as data encrypted with that phone's keys or your ad choices: the app will ask you again on the new phone.

What you choose to share

  • Sharing. A note or a photo only leaves your phone when you share it, with the app you choose on Android's screen.
  • Save a backup. All your notes, with their photos, in a file saved wherever you choose on Android's screen. If you add a password, it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and nobody can open it without the password: if you forget it, there is no way to recover it, not even for us. If you save it in a folder that syncs with a cloud (for example, Google Drive), the syncing is done by the service you chose, under its own terms.
  • Automatic backup. If you turn it on, a backup is saved when you open the app, every day or every week, in the folder you choose. If you set a password, it is fully encrypted; that password is stored only on your phone, protected by the Android keystore. Without a password, anyone who can open that folder can read it, and locked notes are left out. The last 7 are kept.
  • Export notes. Each note as a .md or .txt file, inside a .zip saved wherever you choose.
  • Import notes. If you import a ColorNote backup, a Google Keep export (Google Takeout) or .txt or .md files, Cuadernín reads on your phone the files you choose or share with it, saves the notes on your phone and deletes the temporary copy. If the ColorNote backup has a password, the one you type is only used at that moment to open it and is not stored. Data about the people you shared notes with is not imported. Nothing is sent to JR Soft or to third parties. ColorNote is a trademark of Social & Mobile, Inc., and Google Keep, of Google LLC; Cuadernín is not affiliated with them.
  • Write to us. It opens your email app with a message addressed to us, which is only sent if you send it. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us; we use them only to reply to you, and we delete the message once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after receiving it.
  • Links. A link you write in a note opens in your browser only when you tap it.
  • Spelling and keyboard. “Check spelling” uses the phone's own spell checker. What you type goes through your keyboard, as in any app: if your keyboard has writing tools, they belong to the keyboard and follow its own terms.

Data that does leave your phone

The app sums it up in Settings → What leaves your phone.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and is supported by unobtrusive Google AdMob ads, which only appear in the notes list (a banner at the bottom and, now and then, an ad between the notes): never while you write, in the widgets or in full screen. Ad requests carry nothing from your notes: not the title, not the text, not the labels. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers (T rating).

As with any app that uses AdMob, Google's SDK collects the Android advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads), the app set identifier (app set ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), your interactions with the app and with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to serve and measure advertising, for statistics and to prevent fraud, under its own policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before serving personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you will see non-personalized advertising (or limited advertising, without identifiers, if you also decline the use of device storage) and the app works exactly the same.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ratings and Google Play

Every now and then, after a good moment (for example, when you finish a checklist or save a backup), the app may ask you for a rating through Google Play's official window, which is managed by Google. “Rate the app” and “More apps by JR Soft” open Google Play; their links carry a campaign parameter that only says they come from Cuadernín, with no data about you. “Recommend the app” opens Android's share screen with a link to Cuadernín on Google Play, which is only sent if you send it and carries the same kind of parameter, with none of your data. To show you only the apps available in your country, “More apps by JR Soft” checks on the phone the country of your SIM card or of the system region; that information does not leave your phone.

IP address

As with any app that connects to the internet, the Google services mentioned above receive your IP address when connecting. This app has no servers of its own, so it keeps no logs of any kind.

Legal basis and retention

Legal basis. What is processed on your phone does not reach JR Soft. For what does leave it: (1) personalized advertising and the use of device storage for advertising purposes in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent, which you give or refuse in Google's form and can withdraw in Settings → Ad privacy options (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 22(2) LSSI-CE); (2) non-personalized advertising, its measurement and fraud prevention: the legitimate interest in funding a free app and protecting it from fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); (3) if you write to us (for example with “Write to us”): the legitimate interest in replying to you and solving what you tell us about (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes the AdMob data as an independent controller, under its own policies.

Data stored on your phone remains there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data. Backups you save outside the app stay where you put them until you delete them yourself. This app keeps no data outside your phone, except the messages you send us, which are deleted once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after we receive them; Google keeps its own data according to its policies.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the GDPR grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In practice, this app keeps none of your data outside your phone except the messages you send us (for those, write to the contact address); AdMob data is processed by Google, with whom you can exercise your rights (policies.google.com/privacy). For what is on your phone:

  • Access and portability: share your notes, export them to .md or .txt, or save a backup of all of them from Settings.
  • Rectification: correct your notes in the app itself.
  • Erasure: delete a note, empty the trash or use Settings → Delete all my data; you can also uninstall the app. Backups you saved outside the app are yours: delete them where you saved them. There is no need to ask anyone.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent: change your advertising choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can also write to the contact address or lodge a complaint with your country's supervisory authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

US residents

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to ask that your personal data not be sold or shared for targeted advertising. You can do this in the app: Settings → Ad privacy options → “Don't sell or share my data”. From then on, you will only see non-personalized ads. The app does not send sensitive data to anyone; your notes stay on your phone. For ads, Google's SDK uses device identifiers and an approximate location (city level). For any other request about your data, write to us at demual@gmail.com.

Children

Cuadernín is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at anyone under 16. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers (rating T). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; the technical data sent by AdMob is the data described above.
